2.2. PASSWORD RESET SCREEN FLOW (URL: /reset-password)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 User clicks link in their email inbox
         |
         v
 Opens `/reset-password` in browser
         |
         v
 [ResetPasswordPage]
   - Validates secure reset token from URL
   - Input: New Password
   - Input: Confirm New Password
         |
         v
 Clicks "Update Password"
         |
   +-----+-----+
   |           |
   v           v
Success     Failure (Mismatch or Expired link)
   |           |
   |           v
   |        Show error message with "Request a new link" button
   v
Redirects to `/` with Success notification -> User logs in with new password

2.3. STAFF INVITATION ACCEPTANCE FLOW (URL: /accept-invite)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 Manager invites employee -> Employee clicks link in welcome email

2.5. CUSTOMER CLOUD WEB ORDERING FLOW (URL: /qr/:id -> /order)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 Customer scans table QR code (e.g. /qr/TBL-102)
         |
         v
 [QrRedirect Page]
   - Resolves Table ID & Branch ID
   - Forwards browser to `/order?branch=xxx&table=xxx`
         |
         v
 [TableOrderPage - Cloud Web App View]
   +-----------------------------------------------------------------------+
   | Header: Restaurant Brand Logo, Table Number Badge ("Table 04")        |
   +-----------------------------------------------------------------------+
   | Category Tabs: [All]  [Popular]  [Mains]  [Drinks]  [Desserts]        |
   +-----------------------------------------------------------------------+
   | Search & Filter Bar: Quick search by dish name                        |
   +-----------------------------------------------------------------------+
   | Food Card Grid: Photo, Name, Price, "Add to Cart" Button              |
   +-----------------------------------------------------------------------+
   | Sticky Bottom Bar: Cart Summary (3 Items - RM 42.00) [View Order]     |
   +-----------------------------------------------------------------------+
         |
         +---> Click Food Card ===> [Item Customization Drawer]
         |                           - Size / Portion Options
         |                           - Flavor / Modifier Checkboxes
         |                           - Special Notes TextArea
         |                           - [Add to Cart] Button
         |
         `---> Click [View Order] ===> [Order Review Slide-over]
                                        - List of items with +/- counters
                                        - Subtotal, Tax (SST), Service Charge
                                        - [Submit Order to Kitchen] Button
                                              |
                                              v
                                        Order saved in Cloud DB
                                        Live Status screen: "Order Placed"

4. FEATURE SCREEN-BY-SCREEN FLOWCHARTS
========================================================================================

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1. DASHBOARD SCREEN FLOW (activeTab = 'dashboard')
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 [Dashboard Screen]
   ├── Top KPI Cards: Today's Revenue | Total Orders | Avg Basket Size | Active Tables
   ├── Time Range Filter: [Today] [Yesterday] [Last 7 Days] [This Month]
   ├── Hourly Sales Chart: Visual bars showing peak ordering hours
   ├── Popular Items Ranking: Top 5 dishes sold today
   └── Recent Live Transactions Table
         |
         +---> Click "View All Reports" ──> Switches activeTab to 'reports'
         +---> Click "Manage Menu"      ──> Switches activeTab to 'menu'
         `---> Click "View Tables"      ──> Switches activeTab to 'tables_qr'


----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.2. REPORTS & ANALYTICS SCREEN FLOW (activeTab = 'reports')
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 [Reports Screen]
   ├── Global Report Header: Date Picker | Branch Selector | Export (PDF / Excel)
   ├── Section Navigation Tabs:
   │     ├── [Sales Revenue]       ──> Daily/Weekly charts, net sales, discounts
   │     ├── [Payments]            ──> Cash vs Card vs QR e-Wallet breakdown
   │     ├── [Menu Insights]       ──> High-profit vs Low-profit matrix
   │     ├── [Inventory Stock]     ──> COGS (Cost of Goods Sold) vs Food Waste
   │     ├── [Staff Shift]         ──> Cashier shifts, cash variance in drawer
   │     ├── [Table & Customer]    ──> Table turnover speed & average spend
   │     ├── [Session Reports]     ──> Live shift totals
   │     └── [End-of-Day (EOD)]    ──> Official Day Close & Z-Report
   │
   ├── Click any transaction row ───> Opens [Transaction Receipt Modal]
   │                                  (Full itemized bill, tax breakdown, reprint)
   │
   ├── Click "Generate EOD" ────────> Opens [EOD Report Receipt Modal]
   │                                  (Daily summary slip ready for thermal print)
   │
   └── Click "AI Insights" Button ──> Slides open [AI Insight Drawer]
                                      (AI analysis of sales trends & recommendations)


----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.3. MENU MANAGEMENT SCREEN FLOW (activeTab = 'menu')
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 [Menu Management Screen]
   ├── Category Header Pills: [All] [Beverages] [Mains] [Desserts] [+ Add Category]
   ├── Search Bar & Filter Options
   ├── Food Items Grid / Table
   │     ├── Item Thumbnail, Name, SKU, Price, Cost Margin
   │     ├── Availability Toggle (Instant In-Stock <-> Out-of-Stock switch)
   │     └── Action Buttons: [Edit] [Modifiers] [Delete]
   │
   ├── Click [+ Add Item] or [Edit] ─> Opens [Item Editor Modal]
   │                                   - Upload food photo
   │                                   - Title, Description, Base Price, Cost
   │                                   - Tax Category (SST exempt / standard)
   │                                   - Save button -> Refreshes menu
   │
   └── Click [Modifiers] ────────────> Opens [Modifier Group Modal]
                                       - Sugar Level: 0%, 50%, 100%
                                       - Ice Level: None, Normal, Extra
                                       - Add-ons: Extra Cheese (+RM 2.00)


----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.4. INVENTORY SCREEN FLOW (activeTab = 'inventory')
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 [Inventory Screen]
   ├── Summary Cards: Total Items Tracked | Low Stock Alerts | Out of Stock Items
   ├── Ingredient Stock Table (Item, Category, Current Quantity, Unit, Par Level)
   ├── Low-Stock Indicator (Turns Red when Quantity < Par Level)
   │
   ├── Click [+ Add Stock Item] ─────> Opens [New Ingredient Modal]
   │
   ├── Click [Adjust Stock] ─────────> Opens [Stock Adjustment Modal]
   │                                   - Reason: Wastage, Spoilage, Manual Count
   │                                   - Add/Subtract Quantity
   │
   └── Click [Suppliers Tab] ────────> Opens Supplier Directory & Purchase History


----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.5. TABLES & QR CODE GENERATOR SCREEN FLOW (activeTab = 'tables_qr')
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 [Tables & QR Screen]
   ├── Floor / Zone Selector: [Main Hall] [Outdoor Patio] [Private Room]
   ├── Table Grid View (Interactive Table Cards with Live Status Colors)
   │
   ├── Click [+ Add Table] ──────────> Creates new table (e.g. "Table 12", Capacity: 4)
   │
   ├── Click Table Card ─────────────> Opens [Table Details Drawer]
   │                                   - Current active order items
   │                                   - Table bill total
   │                                   - [Clear / Free Table] button
   │
   └── Click [QR Code Button] ───────> Opens [QR Generator Modal]
                                       - Shows scannable QR Code
                                       - Direct Order URL preview
                                       - [Download PNG] Button
                                       - [Print Table Sticker Sheet] Button


----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.6. STAFF & USERS SCREEN FLOW (activeTab = 'users')
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 [Staff & Users Screen]
   ├── Employee Directory Table (Name, Email, Role, Branch, Status)
   │
   ├── Click [+ Invite Staff] ───────> Opens [Invite Employee Modal]
   │                                   - Staff Email & Full Name
   │                                   - Role Selection: [Cashier] [Manager] [Chef]
   │                                   - Send Invitation Email
   │
   ├── Click [Change Role] ──────────> Role selection dropdown
   │
   └── Click [Reset PIN] ────────────> Opens [4-Digit POS PIN Modal]
                                       - Set quick authorization PIN


----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.7. MARKETING SCREENS FLOW (activeTab = 'promotions' | 'loyalty')
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 [Promotions Screen (activeTab = 'promotions')]
   ├── Active Discounts Table (Code, Discount %, Valid Dates, Usage Count)
   └── Click [+ New Promo] ──────────> Opens [Promo Creator Modal]
                                       - Percentage (e.g. 15% off) or Fixed (RM 5 off)
                                       - Minimum spend condition
                                       - Date & Time schedule (e.g. Happy Hour)

 [Loyalty Screen (activeTab = 'loyalty')]
   ├── Tier Configuration: Bronze, Silver, Gold, VIP
   ├── Earning Rule: Spend RM 1.00 = 1 Loyalty Point
   └── Reward Catalog: Redeem 100 Points = Free Iced Latte


----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.8. COMPLIANCE SCREENS FLOW (activeTab = 'lhdn' | 'tax' | 'audit')
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 [LHDN E-Invoice Screen (activeTab = 'lhdn')]
   ├── Malaysian MyInvois Integration Status (Connected / Synced)
   ├── E-Invoice Ledger Table (UUID, Invoice Date, Buyer Name, Status, Tax Amount)
   ├── Click [Submit Consolidated] ──> Groups monthly B2C sales into 1 LHDN submission
   └── Click [View Validation] ──────> Shows LHDN QR Code & Digital Signature Verification

 [Tax Management Screen (activeTab = 'tax')]
   ├── Sales & Service Tax (SST) Rate Input (e.g. 6% or 8%)
   ├── Service Charge Rate Input (e.g. 10%)
   └── Tax Registration Number (SST ID) Input

 [Audit Logs Screen (activeTab = 'audit')]
   └── Immutable Event Log (Timestamp, User Email, Action, Old Value, New Value)


----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.9. CLOUD SYNC SCREEN FLOW (activeTab = 'cloud_sync')
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 [Cloud Sync Screen]
   ├── Connected Terminals List (Counter POS 01, Handheld Tablet 02)
   ├── Connection Status Badge (Online / Offline / Syncing)
   ├── Pending Sync Queue (Offline orders waiting to upload)
   └── Click [Force Full Sync] ──────> Triggers immediate bidirectional database sync


----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.10. SETTINGS & CUSTOMIZATION SCREEN FLOW (activeTab = 'settings')
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 [Settings Screen]

   │     ├── [Receipt Customization] ──> Header text, Footer message, Logo toggle
   │     ├── [Printer Settings]      ──> Thermal Receipt & Kitchen IP Printers
   │     └── [Operating Hours]       ──> Open/Close times per day of week
   │
   └── Click [Save Changes] ─────────> Updates settings in Cloud DB + Instant live preview

6. AI ASSISTANT OVERLAY & DEEP-LINKING FLOW
========================================================================================

 On ANY page in the Cloud backoffice:
   Customer or Manager sees floating AI Assistant button in bottom corner
         |
         v
   Click Button ──> Slides open [AIAssistant Drawer]
         |
         +---> User asks: "How do I change the receipt footer message?"
         |       |
         |       v
         |     AI answers explanation + displays clickable shortcut button
         |       |
         |       v
         |     User clicks shortcut button
         |       |
         |       v
         |     Cloud instantly switches activeTab to 'settings' and smooth-scrolls
         |     directly to the Receipt Configuration card!
         |
         +---> User asks: "Show me today's best selling drink"
                 |
                 v
               AI queries report cache and summarizes top drink sales directly
